How to read this

Overall conversion is closed people ÷ leads received for the selected received-date cohort. Every card, chart and table uses that same filtered set of FUB people.

Use Original receiving agent for lead-assignment decisions. The current-agent view is useful for workload and follow-up ownership.

Appointment metrics include FUB appointments that carry a person link. They may trail contact and deal-stage coverage while historical appointments are reconciled.
