TDG Agent Operations SMS Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 1, 2026

The Delia Group offers an optional SMS program for affiliated agents who affirmatively enroll to receive operational document and transaction workflow messages. Messages may include signature-status updates, missing-information notices, review requests, completion notices, and related two-way support. This initial program is not used for consumer marketing or lead-nurture messages.

Participation and message frequency

Participation is optional. Message frequency varies according to active document and transaction activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. To request assistance, reply HELP or contact info@thedeliagroup.com or (248) 244-0703. After opting out, no further program messages will be sent unless the recipient affirmatively opts back in.

Privacy

Mobile opt-in information and consent records will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers solely as necessary to operate, secure, and support the messaging program, or as required by law. Mobile opt-in consent will not be transferred to third parties for their independent use.

Security and appropriate use

The program uses reasonable safeguards and limits message content to operational status information. Recipients should not send passwords, account credentials, financial information, or complete transaction documents by SMS.

Enrollment consent is not a condition of affiliation or employment. The Delia Group may modify or discontinue the program. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date and, where appropriate, renewed consent.

The Delia Group's general Privacy Policy is available at https://thedeliagroup.com/privacy-policy.
